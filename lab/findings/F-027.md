## Analysis

| | Rule A (Code A.4, first sentence) | Rule B (Code A.4 "SHALL NOT", with SCOPE §5) |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | Snap Protocol and all Kill Switches | Mercy |
| Object | suspension | its duration |
| Scope | the listed events | the declared Crisis Scope |
| Conditions | an eruption > VEI-6, an asteroid > 200 m, a pandemic > 15 %, a PTL-verified natural drop of B(t) | a Crisis Scope entered through the CRISIS state machine |
| Modality | automatic, "for 180 days" | prohibition |

- **Joint compliance.** Both hold when CRISIS declares a Crisis Scope of at least 180 days at the event.
  They cannot both hold when the declared scope is shorter, or when none is declared, if "for 180 days"
  is a fixed duration.  If it is a maximum, they always can.
- **Priority.** A.4 itself places Mercy "strictly within the boundaries defined by DKP-7-SCOPE-001".
  Nothing orders the two sentences of A.4.
- **Result.** It depends on the reading, on two points:
  - Is "for 180 days" fixed or a maximum?
  - Does Mercy require a Crisis Scope declared by CRISIS?  CRISIS §6 lists Crisis Mercy Mode among the
    actions allowed "During Crisis mode"; the Code says "automatically".
