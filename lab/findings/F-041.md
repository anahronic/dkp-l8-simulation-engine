# F-041 — ERROR, UPGRADE and CRISIS require an "explicit compatibility declaration" without saying with what

**Classification:** CONFIRMED_FINDING (kind `missing_definition`, effect `reading_dependent`).  Open question Q-060.
**Found by:** module `edition_finality`.  **Regression:** `tests/lab/test_findings.py::test_f041_unnamed_compatibility`.

**Searched** (all 39 pinned documents):
- Terms: "compatibility declaration", "compatible with", "Compatibility:".
- 16 finality clauses require a compatibility declaration:
  - 12 name the documents by identifier: POLICE, ETRANS, CULTURE, EDU, HABITAT, INFO, EXIT, INTEGRATION, PRIVACY, TRANSPARENCY, AUDIT, INTEROP;
  - 1 names them by a property: SCOPE, "across all dependent protocols" (F-042);
  - 3 name nothing: ERROR, UPGRADE, CRISIS.
- UPGRADE §5 lists the contents of a proposal and does not mention compatibility.
- DEFENSE §0 and TRANSPORT Appendix A.4 list documents they are "compatible with".  The three clauses do not refer to that form.

## Effect

A gate can tell whether a compatibility declaration exists.  It cannot tell whether the declaration is the one required:
- a declaration naming one document,
- a declaration naming every document,
- a declaration naming none,

all satisfy the literal text.  For the other 13 clauses the required content is fixed (for SCOPE, up to F-042).

## What the lab does

`edition_finality` treats a missing declaration as a fixed failure: the gate refuses the change.  When a declaration exists:
- the check is UNKNOWN;
- the declared targets are recorded in the evidence (`declared_targets`).

## Question (Q-060)

With which documents must a modification of ERROR, UPGRADE or CRISIS declare compatibility?  Possible answers include:
- its dependencies;
- the documents that depend on it;
- the previous edition;
- a fixed list, as in the other clauses.
