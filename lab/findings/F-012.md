## Texts

- EXIT §4 (EXIT-4-03): exit "SHALL NOT be revoked or suspended."
- EXIT §11 (EXIT-11-01): exit during a declared crisis "SHALL remain valid, SHALL NOT be nullified or
  prohibited"; only "Temporary procedural adjustments MAY occur … to prevent immediate physical harm".
- SCOPE §9 (SCOPE-9-03): exit "SHALL NOT be suspended except temporarily within Crisis Scope to
  preserve immediate life."
- SCOPE §2: "In case of conflict, DKP-7-SCOPE-001 prevails."

SCOPE permits a suspension that EXIT §4 forbids; whether a "temporary procedural adjustment"
(EXIT §11) may amount to a suspension is not said.

## Options for the author

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|

| C. Define "procedural adjustment" so that it excludes suspension and keep SCOPE's exception for life only | both texts need a definition |
