# F-012 — Exit in a crisis: SCOPE permits a temporary suspension that EXIT forbids

**Classification:** INTERPRETATION_GAP — difference in granted authority (kind `authority_difference`).
Reclassified from CONFIRMED_FINDING after review (2026-10-08): the requirements are compatible.  Open
question Q-032 (rephrased).
**Found by:** module `exit_integration` (S-LAB-07).  **Regression:** `tests/lab/test_findings.py`,
`tests/lab/test_exit_integration_module.py::test_exit_timing_and_crisis_readings`.

## Analysis

| | EXIT §4, §11 | SCOPE §9 |
|---|---|---|
| Text | "SHALL NOT be revoked or suspended." / "SHALL NOT be blocked by economic status, political conflict, or crisis conditions," / during a crisis exit "SHALL remain valid", only "Temporary procedural adjustments MAY occur" | "exit SHALL NOT be suspended except temporarily within Crisis Scope to preserve immediate life." |
| Subject, object | exit; suspension or revocation | exit; suspension |
| Scope, conditions | every exit, including crisis conditions | Crisis Scope, temporarily, to preserve immediate life |
| Modality | prohibition without exception | prohibition with an exception — a permission inside it |

**Joint compliance:** possible.  Never suspending exit satisfies both texts, and no text makes suspension
obligatory.  The earlier record treated the existence of the exception as a conflict; it is not one.

**What differs:** the authority granted.  SCOPE §9 permits what EXIT §4 forbids.  SCOPE §2 says
"In case of conflict, DKP-7-SCOPE-001 prevails", but whether a permission against a prohibition is a
"conflict" in that sense is not said.  If it is, a Crisis may suspend exit within the narrow exception; if
not, EXIT's stricter prohibition stands.
