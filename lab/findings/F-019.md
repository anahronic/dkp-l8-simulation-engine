# F-019 — An asset revocation has two dispute clocks: from execution (ASSETS) and from recording (ERROR)

## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| ASSETS §8.3(a) | "Dispute Window: Revocation may be disputed within thirty (30) DTI-Days of execution under DKP-4-ERROR-001." |
| ERROR §7 | "be submitted within 30 DTI-Days from the moment the decision was recorded in PTL," |
| ERROR §7 | "Errors of category E1 (Oracle / Physical Truth error) are not subject to any statute of limitations." |

Both windows are 30 DTI-Days.  ASSETS counts from the execution of the revocation, ERROR from the
recording of the decision, and the ASSETS dispute runs "under DKP-4-ERROR-001".  When the revocation is
executed on the day the Justice outcome is recorded, the clocks coincide and nothing is open.  The texts
do not say whether execution can come later (an execution delay, or a PTL halt that defers every asset
decision under ASSETS §2) nor which clock governs then.  No text is contradicted; the result depends on a
reading.

## Question (Q-038)

When a revocation is executed later than the decision is recorded, does the 30-day dispute window run
from execution (ASSETS §8.3(a)) or from recording (ERROR §7)?
