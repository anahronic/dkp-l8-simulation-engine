# F-035 — SCOPE §8: the Scope Audit has a trigger but no procedure, no end and no stated object of confirmation

**Classification:** CONFIRMED_FINDING (kind `missing_definition`).  Open question Q-054.
**Found by:** module `scope_gate`.  **Regression:** `tests/lab/test_findings.py::test_f035_scope_audit`,
`tests/lab/test_scope_gate_module.py::test_scope_audit_blocks_and_stays_open`.

## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| SCOPE §8 | "Any claimed divergence between PTL sensor outputs and observable physical reality, reported by a threshold number of Subjects, SHALL trigger a Scope Audit." |
| SCOPE §8 | "SHALL block transition from S0 to S1," |
| SCOPE §8 | "SHALL require confirmation by independent PTL nodes," |
| SCOPE §8 | "SHALL reject escalation based on single-sensor classes, isolated regions, or non-reproducible signals." |

**Searched** (all 39 pinned documents):
- Terms: "Scope Audit", "threshold number of Subjects", "independent PTL nodes".
- Each occurs once, in SCOPE §8.
- AUDIT (DKP-8-AUDIT-001) does not mention a Scope Audit.

What is missing:
1. **Procedure.** Who conducts the audit, and how.
2. **End.** When the audit closes, and therefore when the block on S0 → S1 lifts.
3. **Object of confirmation.** Does "confirmation by independent PTL nodes" confirm the claimed
   divergence, the escalation, or the audit's conclusion?
4. **Threshold.** The number of Subjects that triggers the audit is not given and not assigned to L8.

## Effect on a deterministic implementation

Blocking every S0 → S1 transition while the audit is open satisfies all three requirements.  Because no
end is defined, a single audit blocks escalation for the rest of the run.  An escalation confirmed by
independent PTL nodes during the audit is refused, and SCOPE-8-04 is reported as UNKNOWN.
`python -m lab run lab/findings/F-035.request.json --out f035.zip` (audit opened on day 90 by 500 reports, declared threshold 300):

| Variant | Escalation | Executed | SCOPE-8-03 | SCOPE-8-04 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `before_audit` | day 88, independent PTL | yes | NOT_APPLICABLE | NOT_APPLICABLE |
| `confirmed_during_audit` | day 95, independent PTL | no | PASS | UNKNOWN |

## Question (Q-054)

1. Who conducts a Scope Audit, and when does it end?
2. What does "confirmation by independent PTL nodes" confirm?
3. How many reporting Subjects trigger the audit?
