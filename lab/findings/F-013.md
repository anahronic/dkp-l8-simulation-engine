## Texts

- INTEROP §7: technical interoperability includes "oracle mirroring".
- INTEROP §9: if an external system becomes unreliable, "DKP internal execution MUST remain
  unaffected" and "Interop failure SHALL NOT trigger Crisis or Mercy mechanisms."
- CRISIS §4: crisis SHALL be entered on "verified loss or divergence of PTL outputs beyond
  correction tolerance".  The tolerance is not defined (CRISIS §6 forbids numbers; SIMULATION has none).
- ORACLE §6 lists Orbital, Ground and Autonomous classes; whether a commercial or foreign provider
  mirrored through INTEROP may be one of them is not said.
