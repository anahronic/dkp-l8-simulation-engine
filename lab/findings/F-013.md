# F-013 — A failing external oracle mirror: no conflict between INTEROP and CRISIS (lab premise withdrawn)

**Classification:** IMPLEMENTATION_BUG (semantic verification 2026-10-07).  The conflict between
INTEROP §9 and CRISIS §4 recorded earlier came from the lab, not from the texts.  Open question Q-033
withdrawn.
**Found by:** module `interop_failure` (S-LAB-10).  **Regression:** `tests/lab/test_findings.py`,
`tests/lab/test_interop_failure_module.py::test_source_roles_do_not_collide`.

## What the lab did

Module version 1.0.0 counted the sources of an external oracle mirror among PTL sources.  When the
provider failed, PTL "lost" a share of its sources beyond the declared tolerance, and the lab then
showed that CRISIS §4 (enter crisis) and INTEROP §9 (no crisis from interop failure) could not both
hold.

## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| INTEROP §3 | "**External System** — any legal, economic, technical, or informational system not governed by DKP protocols." |
| INTEROP §7 | "Black-box or proprietary control logic SHALL NOT be trusted as authoritative inputs." |
| INTEROP §8 | "External information MAY be ingested only as:" / "- untrusted inputs," / "- I1 (Unverified Claims) under DKP-5-INFO-001." |
| INTEROP §8 | "External consensus, authority, or institutional validation SHALL NOT substitute PTL verification." |
| INTEROP §9 | "Interop failure SHALL NOT trigger Crisis or Mercy mechanisms." |
| CRISIS §4 | "Crisis mode SHALL be entered when one or more of the following conditions are PTL-confirmed:" … "- verified loss or divergence of PTL outputs beyond correction tolerance," |

A source is either an External System (not governed by DKP protocols; its readings enter only as
untrusted I1 and cannot substitute PTL verification) or a source governed by DKP protocols, i.e. a
registered PTL source under ORACLE.  It cannot be both.  So:

- the outage of an external mirror removes no PTL output: INTEROP §9 applies, CRISIS §4 is not
  triggered;
- the outage of registered PTL sources (whoever operates them) is a loss of PTL outputs: CRISIS §4
  applies, and it is not an interop failure, so INTEROP §9 does not apply.

The two rules never meet.  The lab's premise "an external mirror feeds PTL" is itself forbidden by
INTEROP §8.

## Fix in the lab (module 1.1.0)

- Input `mirror_role` (source `user`, a fact of the configuration, not a reading):
  `untrusted_interop` (default) or `registered_ptl_source`.
- The former premise is now the counterexample fault `mirror_feeds_ptl` (fails INTEROP-8-01 and
  INTEROP-8-02); entering crisis on the mirror outage is `crisis_from_interop` (fails INTEROP-9-02,
  INTEROP-9-04); ignoring a registered PTL loss is `no_crisis_on_ptl_loss` (fails CRISIS-4-01).
- INTEROP-9-04 is checked by comparison with the run without the interop failure; CRISIS-4-01 from
  the declared inputs, counting registered sources only.

## What remains open (not a finding)

The CRISIS §4 "correction tolerance" has no value in the corpus (L8 parameter; see the calibration
map in `lab/registry/calibration_map.json`).  Whether INTEROP §7 "oracle mirroring" means mirroring
DKP oracles outward or external oracles inward does not change the result: inward data are I1 by §8.
