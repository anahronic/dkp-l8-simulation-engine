## Analysis

| | Rule A (IMPACT §4, §7; IDENTITY §11) | Rule B (ORACLE §1, §4.1, §5.3) |
|---|---|---|
| Subject | IMPACT channels; IDENTITY attribution | PTL outputs |
| Object | cognitive integrity and cultural continuity measured from PTL data | outputs concerning cultural or social harm; anything that is not physical state |
| Scope | every evaluated action | every PTL output |
| Modality | the channels are "normatively declared"; their only admissible source is the PTL | prohibition; physical state only |

- **Joint compliance** is possible only if a physically measurable proxy for cognitive integrity or
  cultural continuity exists that is neither an output "concerning cultural harm" nor a semantic state
  (ORACLE §5.2).  No document names such a proxy.  IDENTITY §11's indicators ("language coherence",
  "disinformation persistence", "loss of epistemic capacity") are not physical quantities.
- **Priority.** No text ranks IMPACT §4 or IDENTITY §11 against ORACLE §5.3.
- **Result.** It depends on the reading.  The two channels can be fed only if the PTL may emit physical
  proxies of cultural and cognitive states.
