# F-040 — Three protocols protect individual data "under DKP-7-SCOPE-001", which contains no data-protection rules

**Classification:** CONFIRMED_FINDING (kind `broken_reference`).  Open question Q-059.
**Found by:** module `habitat_dignity`.  **Regression:** `tests/lab/test_findings.py::test_f040_scope_data_protection`.

## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| HABITAT §12 | "Individual-level exposure data SHALL be protected under DKP-7-SCOPE-001." |
| EDU §10 (individual learning data) | "- SHALL be protected under DKP-7-SCOPE-001," |
| AUDIT §6 | "- selectively anonymized under DKP-7-SCOPE-001." |
| PRIVACY §7 | "The following MAY be exposed only in anonymized or aggregated form:" |
| PRIVACY §8 | "## 8. Anonymity Guarantees" |

**Searched** (all 39 pinned documents):
- In SCOPE: "protect", "individual", "privacy", "anonym", "personal data", "exposure".
- SCOPE uses "individual" once, in S3: "moral valuation or ranking of individual human lives".
- SCOPE contains no rule on protecting, minimizing, aggregating or anonymizing data.
- Those rules are in PRIVACY: §7 aggregated exposure, §8 anonymity guarantees, §10 prohibited practices.

**Reference chain:**
- HABITAT §12, EDU §10 (individual learning data) and AUDIT §6 all point to SCOPE.
- SCOPE §10 limits transparency obligations; it says nothing on protecting individual data.
- None of the three protocols names PRIVACY.

## Effect

A literal implementation of "protected under DKP-7-SCOPE-001" finds no rule to apply.  Reading PRIVACY
in its place is an assumption.  It is likely what the authors meant, but the texts do not say so.
