## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| ORACLE §11 | "Upon a Divergence Event:" / "all dependent state-based transactions SHALL be frozen." |
| ORACLE §11 | "Maximum arbitration window: 14 days." |
| ORACLE §11 | "Resolution requires:" / "convergence within the threshold, or" / "exclusion of faulty or compromised sources." |
| ORACLE §16 | "\| Arbitration window \| 14 days \|" |

The window is a maximum, and the two ways to resolve are named, but the text defines neither how a
faulty or compromised source is identified and excluded (by whom, on what evidence) nor what state
follows when the window passes without convergence (halt, continued freeze, forced exclusion).  When
the physical disagreement persists, no deterministic implementation can keep the window without
choosing one of these itself.  The lab does not choose: it reports the exceeded window.

## Options for the author

A. Define the state after the window (e.g. Systemic Halt under ORACLE §13, or continued freeze).
B. Define the exclusion procedure (criteria, responsible component) so that resolution within 14 days
   is always possible.
