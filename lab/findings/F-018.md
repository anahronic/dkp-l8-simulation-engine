# F-018 — ORACLE §11: nothing says what follows when the 14-day arbitration window ends unresolved

**Classification:** CONFIRMED_FINDING (kind `missing_definition`).  Revised after review (2026-10-08): the
lab no longer reports a reference FAIL.  It now measures the arbitration window, not the duration of the
disagreement, and reports the transition after an unresolved window as UNKNOWN.  Open questions Q-005
(consequence of the window), Q-010 ("dependent state-based transactions").
**Found by:** module `failure_chain` (S-LAB-01).  **Regression:** `tests/lab/test_findings.py::test_f018_window_closes_next_state_undefined`,
`tests/lab/test_failure_chain_module.py` (counterexample `arbitration_overrun`).

## Temporal semantics used by the lab (H-FC-7)

- **Window opens** at the divergence flag (ORACLE §11 "Upon a Divergence Event").
- **Window closes** on convergence within 7 %, on exclusion of the faulty class, or at the latest when 14
  days have passed ("Maximum arbitration window: 14 days"), even if the data still disagree.  Each
  closing is an event in the log, with its outcome.
- **ORACLE-11-03** checks the length of each window: from its opening event to its closing event.  A
  divergence may outlast its window.
- **After an unresolved window**, the next state is not defined.  ORACLE-11-04 reports UNKNOWN.
  Dependent transactions stay frozen while the data disagree (status quo, ETRANS §4.3).  This is a
  declared hypothesis, not a reading of ORACLE.
- **Counterexample** `arbitration_overrun`: the window is kept open after 14 days, and ORACLE-11-03 fails.

`python -m lab run lab/findings/F-018.request.json --out f018.zip` — one oracle class reads 12 % low from hour 24:

## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| ORACLE §11 | "Upon a Divergence Event:" / "all dependent state-based transactions SHALL be frozen." |
| ORACLE §11 | "Maximum arbitration window: 14 days." |
| ORACLE §11 | "Resolution requires:" / "convergence within the threshold, or" / "exclusion of faulty or compromised sources." |
| ORACLE §16 | "\| Arbitration window \| 14 days \|" |
| Code, Appendix A.2 | "Divergence > 7% → automatic arbitration with a $500M stake. All related tranches are frozen until resolution (max 14 days)." |

**Searched** (all 39 pinned documents): arbitration, divergence, exclusion, resolution.  Results:
- The Code (A.2) restates the 14-day maximum and adds a stake.
- INTERNAL-SEC §6 defers to L0 on an arbitration flag.
- INFO's "informational arbitration" is a different mechanism.

No text defines how a faulty or compromised source is identified and excluded (by whom, on what evidence),
nor what state follows a window that ends without convergence or exclusion (halt, continued freeze,
release, forced exclusion).

## Options for the author

A. Define the state after the window (for example Systemic Halt under ORACLE §13, continued freeze, or release).
B. Define the exclusion procedure (criteria, responsible component) so that resolution within 14 days is always possible.
