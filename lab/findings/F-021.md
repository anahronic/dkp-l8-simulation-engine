## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| INTERNAL-SEC §5 | "S3 – Active Compromise Partial system isolation or execution throttling permitted. Maximum duration: 3 DTI-Days." |
| INTERNAL-SEC §5 | "State transitions MUST be evidence-driven, auditable, and automatically reverted to S0 upon expiration of the maximum duration unless escalated." |
| INTERNAL-SEC §5 | "S4 – Systemic Compromise Mandatory escalation to DKP-4-CRISIS-001." |

The maximum durations and the automatic reversion are literal.  What happens next is not: when the
PTL evidence that put the system in S3 (or S2, S1) still holds after the reversion, the text neither
allows nor forbids an immediate new evidence-driven transition.  Escalation to S4 needs systemic
compromise, which a persisting local compromise is not.

## Minimal reproduction

`python -m lab run lab/findings/F-021.request.json --out f021.zip` — an active compromise confirmed
by PTL on days 3–12 (ten days), never systemic:

| Variant (H-IS-2) | States | Days with containment |
|---|---|---:|
| `evidence_driven` (default) | S3 3–5, S0 6, S3 7–9, S0 10, S3 11–12, S0 13 | 8 |
| `episode_once` | S3 3–5, S0 from day 6 | 3 |

Both keep every stated limit.  One reading turns the 3-day limit into repeated pulses; the other leaves
an ongoing compromise uncontained after three days.  The lab does not choose.
