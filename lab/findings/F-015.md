## Texts

- TRANSPORT §7.1: every private kilometre generates "mortality risk, medical system load,
  infrastructure degradation, systemic insurance exposure"; "The Physical Truth Layer SHALL quantify
  this harm as: **RED — Risk Externalization Delta**".
- TRANSPORT §7.2: RED is "computed via DKP-0-ORACLE-001 and DKP-1-IMPACT-001".
- IMPACT §5: "No aggregation across channels is permitted."  IMPACT §10: "No aggregated impact score
  SHALL be produced."  IMPACT §3 excludes "aggregation across channels" from its scope.
- "Systemic insurance exposure" is an economic quantity, not a physical state measured by PTL
  (ORACLE §5.3 excludes non-measurable domains; IMPACT measures differences of physical states).
