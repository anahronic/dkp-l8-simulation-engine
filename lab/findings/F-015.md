# F-015 — TRANSPORT has PTL quantify "systemic insurance exposure"; ORACLE limits PTL to physical state

**Classification:** CONFIRMED_FINDING, scope narrowed by the semantic verification of 2026-10-07.
The part recorded earlier — "a single RED is an aggregated score that IMPACT §5/§10 forbid" — is an
INTERPRETATION_GAP, not a conflict (see "Withdrawn part").  Open question Q-036 (rephrased).
**Found by:** module `transport_capacity` (S-LAB-09).  **Regression:** `tests/lab/test_findings.py`,
`tests/lab/test_transport_capacity_module.py::test_red_insurance_exposure_conflicts_with_oracle`.

## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| TRANSPORT §7.1 | "Every kilometer traveled via private or high-risk transport modes generates measurable probabilistic external harm, including:" … "systemic insurance exposure." |
| TRANSPORT §7.1 | "The Physical Truth Layer SHALL quantify this harm as:" / "RED — Risk Externalization Delta" |
| ORACLE §1 | "The PTL produces physical state only." |
| ORACLE §1 | "The PTL does not:" … "assign value," |

"This harm" includes systemic insurance exposure, a financial quantity.  TRANSPORT §7.1 assigns its
quantification to the Physical Truth Layer; ORACLE §1 says the PTL produces physical state only and
does not assign value.  Followed literally, one of the two is violated.  (Mortality risk, medical
system load and infrastructure degradation can be read as physical or proxy-based quantities under
ORACLE §5.2; the lab does not count them against ORACLE §1.)

## Withdrawn part (interpretation gap)

TRANSPORT §7.2 says RED is "computed via DKP-0-ORACLE-001 and DKP-1-IMPACT-001".  It does not say
that IMPACT produces the single delta.  IMPACT §5 ("No aggregation across channels is permitted.")
and §10 ("No aggregated impact score SHALL be produced.") bind IMPACT's outputs; if RED is assembled
from IMPACT channel outputs elsewhere, nothing is violated.  Where the channels are combined is not
defined (architecture gap), so the lab now reports IMPACT-10-02 as UNKNOWN for a single delta, not FAIL.
