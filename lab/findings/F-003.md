# F-003 — A class slower than its TTL keeps dependent outputs suspended

**Classification:** INTERPRETATION_GAP (semantic verification 2026-10-07): ORACLE §9 says zero-weight conditions "MUST propagate upward as explicit uncertainty, degradation, or halt signals" without mapping them to the EB §5 states; the suspension follows from the lab's mapping H-FC-1.  Open questions Q-003 (architecture definition), Q-008 (normative ambiguity, EB §9 against ORACLE §9).

Data type "Active fire detection" (ORACLE §17: maximum TTL **10 minutes**), 5-minute steps,
3 hours.  Ground sensors every 5 min, autonomous platforms every 10 min, orbital sensing
every 90 min or every 10 min.

| Variant | Suspended steps of 36 | Active steps | Final mode |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `orbital_every_90min` | 32 | 4 | SUSPENDED |
| `orbital_every_10min` | 0 | 36 | ACTIVE |

Each orbital reading is admissible for 10 minutes, then gets weight 0 (ORACLE §9).  Under
the lab's mapping H-FC-1 a non-excluded class with weight 0 makes the output
`DEGRADED_SIGNAL` → `CONDITIONAL`; SUFFICIENT_DATA → DEGRADED_SIGNAL is a listed downgrade
(ETRANS §4.1), so dependent outputs are suspended until the next orbital pass and a
recomputation — about 89 % of the time.

## Chain of provisions

1. ORACLE §17 (ORACLE-17-01) — "Active fire detection | 10 minutes".
2. ORACLE §9 (ORACLE-9-01) — "Data exceeding its TTL SHALL have its confidence weight set to zero."
3. ORACLE §9 (ORACLE-9-03) — "Oracle silence, loss of signal, or zero-weight conditions … MUST
   propagate upward as **explicit uncertainty, degradation, or halt signals**."
4. EB §5 — confidence states SUFFICIENT_DATA / INSUFFICIENT_DATA / DEGRADED_SIGNAL; no mapping
   from ORACLE conditions is given (lab hypothesis H-FC-1).
5. ETRANS §4.1 — "SUFFICIENT_DATA → DEGRADED_SIGNAL" requires revalidation and suspension.
6. EB §9 — "Absence of new data alone does NOT invalidate output if: environment is stable,
   no conflicting signals exist"; "Protocols MUST distinguish between data absence under
   activity [and] data absence under stability."  ORACLE gives no such distinction.

## Options for the author

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| A. A class not expected to report within the TTL (declared cadence) is not "silent" | orbital passes count only when due; needs a registered cadence per class |
| B. Confidence by coverage (ORACLE §4.5, minimum global coverage 80 % in §13) instead of "any class at zero weight" | slow classes lower coverage without forcing DEGRADED_SIGNAL |
| C. Keep the literal reading | data types with short TTL can rely only on classes reporting faster than the TTL |
| D. Apply EB §9 (absence under stability) to PTL data | needs a definition of a stable environment and who detects it |
