## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| Code §14.2 | "14.2. Quadratic Protection: The cost of buying validator votes grows exponentially (Cost = Votes²), making a capital attack economically impossible." |

The title ("Quadratic Protection") and the formula (Votes²) describe a quadratic cost.  The prose says
"exponentially".  A quadratic function is not exponential: for 10, 20 and 40 votes it gives 100, 400
and 1,600, while 2^Votes gives about 10³, 10⁶ and 10¹².

**Effect.** The lab computes with the formula, the only exact statement.  The claim that a capital attack
is "economically impossible" is a claim about outcomes, not a rule; under a quadratic cost a majority
of N votes costs about (N/2)², a finite sum.  The cost is charged per Subject.  Identities that
coordinate are one Subject (IDENTITY §6, H-CE-4).  Without that rule, splitting the purchase across n
identities would divide the cost by n (fault `per_identity_cost`).
