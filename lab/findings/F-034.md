## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| Code §14.2 | "14.2. Quadratic Protection: The cost of buying validator votes grows exponentially (Cost = Votes²), making a capital attack economically impossible." |

The title ("Quadratic Protection") and the formula (Votes²) describe a quadratic cost.  The prose says
"exponentially".  A quadratic function is not exponential: for 10, 20 and 40 votes it gives 100, 400
and 1,600, while 2^Votes gives about 10³, 10⁶ and 10¹².

**Effect.** The lab computes with the formula, the only exact statement.  The claim that a capital attack
is "economically impossible" is a claim about outcomes, not a rule; under a quadratic cost a majority
of N votes costs about (N/2)², a finite sum.

**Who pays is not said.**  IDENTITY §6 makes a coordinating coalition a Subject for the duration of its
coordination, but neither it nor §14.2 says whether Votes² is charged to that collective Subject or
to each identity.  The lab charges the collective Subject by default (H-CE-4, input `pricing_unit`)
and keeps the other unit as a named alternative.  Both apply Votes² to their own unit, so CODEX-14.2-01
passes in both; the totals differ by the number of identities.  IDENTITY-6-02 checks only what IDENTITY
§6 says: that the coalition holds Subject status throughout its coordination (revised after review
КТ4-GPT-03, which found the earlier check tied to the price).
