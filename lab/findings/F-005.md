| Variant | S(t₀) | S(t₁) | Bᵢ | Iᵢ by §5 | Boundary crossing flag (|Iᵢ| ≥ 1) | S(t₁) beyond the level Bᵢ |

| `example_s13` | 426.91 | 427.00 | 450 | 0.0002 | no | no |
| `level_crossed` | 426.91 | 460.00 | 450 | 0.0735 | **no** | **yes** |

1. §5 defines Iᵢ = (Sᵢ(t₁) − Sᵢ(t₀)) / Bᵢ and flags a boundary crossing when |Iᵢ| ≥ 1.
   With Bᵢ declared as a limit ("Reference Bounds Bᵢ — Invariant physical limits", §4), a
   state that moves past the limit (460 > 450) is **not** flagged: the flag needs a change as
   large as the limit itself (here 450 units).
2. The non-normative example of §13 (S(t₀) = 426.91, S(t₁) = 427.00, Bᵢ = 450) states
   "Iᵢ ≈ 0.004".  The §5 formula gives 0.09 / 450 = **0.0002**.  The stated number matches
   0.09 / (450 − 427) ≈ 0.0039, i.e. the change divided by the remaining headroom to the limit —
   a different formula.
