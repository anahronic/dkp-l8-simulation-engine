# F-007 — N_eff and N_eff_min decide the null-output state but are defined nowhere

**Classification:** CONFIRMED_FINDING (semantic verification 2026-10-07) — missing definition: CRISIS PATCH §2 and RESILIENCE §5.1 use N_eff and N_eff_min "(DKP-0-ORACLE-001)"; ORACLE does not contain them.  Open question Q-026 (architecture definition); the value of N_eff_min is an L8 parameter.
**Found by:** module `sensor_loss` (S-LAB-03).  **Regression:** `tests/lab/test_findings.py`, `tests/lab/test_sensor_loss_module.py`.

Five regions of ten sources (ten owners each); region 0 loses all sensors in steps 12–23.

|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `n_eff_min_missing` | owners | not defined | 0 | 0 | UNKNOWN |
| `owners_45` | owners | 45 (declared) | 0 | 12 | PASS |
| `sources_45_sybil` | sources | 45 (declared) | 40 | **0** | PASS |
| `owners_45_sybil` | owners | 45 (declared) | 40 | 12 | PASS |

Without a definition the transition to NO_ENFORCEMENT cannot be computed.  With a declared
threshold, the result depends on how N_eff is counted: counted over sources, forty sources of
one owner keep N_eff above the threshold and hide the loss of a whole region; counted over
owners, they do not.

## Chain of provisions

- CRISIS PATCH §2 (CRISISPATCH-2-01) — trigger "`N_eff < N_eff_min OR oracle consistency invalid`",
  "Reference: DKP-0-ORACLE-001".
- CRISIS PATCH §5.2 — recovery "`If N_eff ≥ N_eff_min AND consistency restored`".
- RESILIENCE §5.1 — "System must remain valid only if N_eff ≥ N_eff_min (DKP-0-ORACLE-001)";
  §8.4 statistical collapse; §5.8 "artificial scaling does not increase influence".
- ORACLE: §8 caps per source and per owner (4 %), §6 independent classes, §13 coverage and
  confidence thresholds — **no N_eff, no N_eff_min**.  A search of all 39 documents finds the
  symbols only in CRISIS PATCH and RESILIENCE.
