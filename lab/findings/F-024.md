## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| AXIOMS §5 | "2. Violation of any axiom invalidates an action regardless of utility score and propagates to higher-layer protocols for enforcement." |
| AXIOMS §5 | "4. Conflicts between axioms are resolved only by higher-priority axioms." |
| AXIOMS §6 | "Lower-priority axioms SHALL NOT override higher-priority ones." |

When at least one action violates no axiom, both rules agree.  When every available action violates some
axiom (a dilemma), rule 2 invalidates all of them, while rule 4 resolves the conflict by priority, i.e.
prefers the action whose violations concern lower-priority axioms.  The text does not say which applies,
nor what "propagates to higher-layer protocols" means when no action remains.
