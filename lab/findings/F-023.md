# F-023 — Axiom A1 "minimum required conditions": every text defers the definition, none gives it

**Classification:** CONFIRMED_FINDING (semantic verification 2026-10-07) — missing definition.  Open question Q-042.

## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| AXIOMS A1 | "The definition of such minimum required conditions is exclusively governed by DKP-4-CRISIS and SHALL NOT be inferred, approximated, or declared by operational, economic, or enforcement protocols." |
| CRISIS §7 | "Interpretation of “minimum required conditions” under Axiom A1 SHALL NOT be inferred, extended, or operationalized by Crisis execution logic or by any operational, economic, or enforcement mechanism." |
| CRISIS §7 | "Such interpretation is permitted only within the Crisis Scope explicitly defined in DKP-7-SCOPE-001." |
| CRISIS §12.2 | outputs that interpret the conditions carry "interpretation_authority: DKP-4-CRISIS-001 (not transferable)" |
| SCOPE | "Crisis Scope is defined and activated only by DKP-4-CRISIS-001," |
| POLICE §5.2 | "Lethal force is permitted only under Axiom A1 minimum-required conditions, as defined exclusively by DKP-4-CRISIS-001." |

AXIOMS assigns the definition to CRISIS.  CRISIS contains no definition: it forbids its own execution logic
and every operational mechanism to operationalize the conditions and permits interpretation only inside a
Crisis Scope, which SCOPE in turn says only CRISIS defines.  The chain closes without a definition and
without a component allowed to apply one.  POLICE makes lethal force depend on it.
