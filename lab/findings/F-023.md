**Searched** (all 39 pinned documents): "minimum required", "minimum-required", "required conditions".
The term occurs in AXIOMS, CRISIS, SCOPE and POLICE only.

What exists:
- the authority is named (CRISIS §12.2);
- the place where the conditions may be interpreted is named (Crisis Scope);
- the ways they may not be obtained are named: not inferred, approximated or operationalized by
  operational, economic or enforcement mechanisms.

What is missing: a reproducible admissibility test, i.e. which evidence and which checks establish that
a given action meets the conditions.  A numeric threshold, a flag such as `minimum_conditions_met`, or the
knowledge of the scenario generator cannot replace it.  The earlier wording ("no component allowed to
apply one") was wrong and is withdrawn.

## What the lab does

The lab checks the declared limits; it never admits the conditions positively.
- `axiom_constraints`: outside a Crisis Scope an action justified by the conditions is refused (axioms are
  "executed, not interpreted", SCOPE), and AXIOMS-…-02 is UNKNOWN.
- `police_antiterror`: lethal force without a CRISIS authorization fails POLICE-5.2-04.  With a declared
  authorization the check is UNKNOWN, never PASS.

## Question (Q-042)

CRISIS is the named authority.  Which evidence and checks establish that an action meets the A1 minimum
required conditions?
