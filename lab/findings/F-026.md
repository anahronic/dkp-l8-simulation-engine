## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| PRIVACY §6 | "The following domains SHALL ALWAYS remain private and non-public:" — medical and biological data, mental and psychological states, intimate relationships, beliefs, private communications |
| PRIVACY §6 | "These domains are non-extractable and non-auditable at the individual level." |
| PRIVACY §7 | "The following MAY be exposed only in anonymized or aggregated form:" — economic activity, resource consumption, mobility, education, platform metrics (the absolute domains are not in this list) |
| TRANSPARENCY §5 | "system-wide metrics and aggregate indicators" are public by default |

The first sentence of §6 keeps the domains "private and non-public" without a level; the second limits
extraction and audit "at the individual level".  §7 names the domains that may be exposed in aggregate
form and does not name the absolute ones, while TRANSPARENCY §5 makes aggregate indicators public by
default.  Whether, for example, aggregate health statistics may be published is not settled.  No text is
contradicted; the result depends on the reading.
