## Chain of provisions

- DEFENSE §5 (DEFENSE-5-01): "**D3 – Existential Threat** … Mandatory escalation to DKP-4-CRISIS-001."
- DEFENSE §9 (DEFENSE-9-01): Defense MUST escalate when PTL confirms axiomatic boundary breach risk.
- CRISIS §4 (CRISIS-4-01): crisis is entered on "sustained D3 state escalation from DKP-3-DEFENSE-001".
- CRISIS §6: "No numerical values, thresholds, coefficients, timers, decay curves, or quantitative
  authority limits MAY be defined in this protocol … SHALL be defined … exclusively under
  DKP-8-SIMULATION-001."  SIMULATION defines none.

Between the first D3 hour and the undefined end of "sustained", DEFENSE requires escalation and
CRISIS does not admit entry; the length of that window is set by a number that does not exist.
