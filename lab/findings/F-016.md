# F-016 — PPDI asks PTL to measure "non-consenting" contact; ORACLE forbids PTL outputs about consent

**Status:** UNRESOLVED — contradiction between protocols (open question Q-037).
**Found by:** module `transport_capacity` (S-LAB-09).  **Regression:** `tests/lab/test_findings.py` (quotes checked in the pinned corpus).

## Texts

- TRANSPORT §4.1: "PPDI SHALL include, at minimum, the following Physical Truth Layer–measurable
  indicators: … prohibition of **forced bodily contact** between non-consenting passengers".
- ORACLE §5.3 (ORACLE-5.3-01): "The PTL SHALL NOT emit outputs concerning: intent, dignity, belief,
  responsibility, consent, moral, cultural, or social harm."
- TRANSPORT §7.1: "The Physical Truth Layer SHALL quantify this harm as: RED", where the harm includes
  "medical system load" and "systemic insurance exposure"; ORACLE §1 limits PTL to physical state and
  §5.3 excludes social harm (see also F-015).

Whether contact is consented is not a physical state; a PTL-measurable indicator of it does not
exist under ORACLE §5.3.  The lab uses standing density above a declared threshold as a physical
stand-in and says so in every result (TRANSPORT-4.1-02).

## Options for the author

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| A. Replace the component by a physical one (standing density, minimum personal space) | measurable; drops the consent element |
| B. Consent enters PPDI from outside PTL (e.g. opt-in seating classes recorded by L5) | PTL stays physical; TRANSPORT names the source |
| C. ORACLE §5.3 admits a defined exception | ORACLE change |
