# F-016 — PPDI "forced bodily contact between non-consenting passengers": how PTL measures it is undefined

**Classification:** INTERPRETATION_GAP (semantic verification 2026-10-07; formerly recorded as a
contradiction between TRANSPORT and ORACLE — withdrawn).  Open question Q-037 withdrawn: the
operationalization of the indicator is a model and calibration choice (L8), not an author decision.
**Found by:** module `transport_capacity` (S-LAB-09).  **Regression:** `tests/lab/test_findings.py` (quotes checked in the pinned corpus).

## Verification against the pinned text

| Place | Literal text |
|---|---|
| TRANSPORT §4.1 | "PPDI SHALL include, at minimum, the following Physical Truth Layer–measurable indicators:" |
| TRANSPORT §4.1 | "prohibition of forced bodily contact between non-consenting passengers," |
| ORACLE §5.3 | "The PTL SHALL NOT emit outputs concerning:" … "consent," |

The earlier record read the indicator as a PTL output about consent.  The text calls the indicator
"Physical Truth Layer–measurable" and its object is *forced bodily contact* — contact imposed by the
physical conditions (crowding density, seat geometry), which is a physical state.  "Non-consenting"
describes the passengers the prohibition protects; it does not require PTL to observe whether anyone
consented.  Read that way, nothing in ORACLE §5.3 is violated.  Only a reading that makes PTL decide
consent conflicts with ORACLE §5.3, and the text does not demand that reading.

What is open is the measurement model: which physical quantity stands for "forced contact" and from
which threshold.  The corpus gives neither (no PPDI formula or minimum either, TRANSPORT §4.2).  That
is a missing model/calibration (category `l8_parameter` / `empirical_model_data`), recorded in
`lab/registry/calibration_map.json`.

## What the lab does

Standing density above a declared threshold (`contact_density_m2`, source `missing`, H-TR-2) is the
physical stand-in; every result says so (TRANSPORT-4.1-02), and ORACLE-5.3-01 checks that no PTL
message concerns consent.
