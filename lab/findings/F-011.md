## Texts

- EXIT §6: "Exit execution occurs after a fixed cooling period, calibrated exclusively under
  DKP-8-SIMULATION-001."  "The cooling period exists to: prevent cascading instability, allow
  settlement of restitution tails, enable orderly disengagement, protect non-exiting parties from
  abrupt dependency loss."
- SCOPE §9 (SCOPE-9-02): "exit SHALL NOT be delayed for optimization or stability goals".
- SCOPE §2: "In case of conflict, DKP-7-SCOPE-001 prevails."
- DKP-8-SIMULATION-001 gives no cooling value; EXIT §6 cannot be followed as written either.

Read with SCOPE §2 ("SCOPE prevails"), a cooling period justified by stability is invalid; read
with EXIT §6, it is mandatory.  Only one stated purpose ("allow settlement of restitution tails")
is not a stability goal.

## Options for the author

| Option | Effect |
|---|---|
| A. Cooling only for settlement of restitution tails and orderly disengagement; drop "prevent cascading instability" and "protect non-exiting parties" | EXIT §6 consistent with SCOPE §9 |
| B. SCOPE §9 admits a bounded, calibrated cooling period | SCOPE change |
| C. Keep both; SCOPE prevails (§2), so stability-motivated cooling is void | EXIT §6 partly inapplicable |
